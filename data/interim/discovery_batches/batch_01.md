### 1. STRUCTURE & FRAMING

*   **Standardized Czech Doxology (Khutbat al-Hajah) Wrapper:**
    Across all sermons in this batch, the Arabic source texts begin immediately with topic introductions or direct Quranic quotes without a top formal doxology. In contrast, every Czech text systematically inserts a standardized intro header and formal doxology (*Khutbat al-Hajah*):
    *   *Czech Intro Pattern:* `Páteční kázání [DD. MM. YYYY] ([Hijri Date]) \n [Title] \n Chvála Alláhu. Jeho uctíváme a Jeho o pomoc prosíme. Dosvědčuji, že není božstva kromě Alláha a že Muhammad (salla lláhu ʻalajhi wa sallam) je Jeho služebník a posel. „Vy, kteří jste uvěřili, chraňte se před trestem Alláha a říkejte slova správná!...“`
    *   *Arabic Direct Starts:* `[2026-11-06]`: "تعالوا نتأمل اليوم نعمة عظيمة..."; `[2026-10-16]`: "من أبرز سمات عصرنا كثرة الأحداث..."; `[2026-09-11]`: "تخيَّل أنك صاحب تجارة..."
*   **Date and Title Formatting:**
    *   Arabic titles include Gregorian dates in parentheses, e.g., `(2026/11/6)` `[2026-11-06]` or `(2026/10/30)` `[2026-10-30]`.
    *   Czech headers convert dates into standard Czech format and add the calculated Hijri date, e.g., `6. 11. 2026 (26. džumáda al-awwal 1448 h )` `[2026-11-06]`.
*   **Structural Reformatting & Numbered Lists:**
    The Czech text frequently introduces numbered lists or explicit structural markers where the Arabic text uses continuous narrative prose or unnumbered ordinal words:
    *   `[2026-10-16]`: Arabic headings like "أولًا: تعميق الإيمان بالقضاء والقدر" and "ثانيًا: لا تجعل الخوف يوقف حياتك" are formatted as clean numbered items in Czech: `1) Víra v osud a předurčenost`, `2) Nenechte se ovládat strachem`.
    *   `[2026-10-02]`: Arabic sections "أولًا: التطفيف مع الخالق", "ثانيًا: التطفيف مع الخَلْق" are converted to `1) Krácení míry vůči Stvořiteli`, `2) Podvádění lidí`.
    *   `[2026-09-04]`: Unnumbered Arabic bullet-like phrases ("دوام المراقبة والاستشعار بمعية الله:", "قطع أسباب المعصية وسد الذرائع:") are converted into a strict numbered list (`1) Neustálá bdělost...`, `2) Odstranit příčiny...`).
*   **Closing Supplications (Du'a):**
    Arabic supplications ending with formal phrases like `آمين يا رب العالمين` are fully translated into Czech:
    *   `[2026-11-06]`: "برحمته يا أرحم الراحمين. آمين يا رب العالمين." $\rightarrow$ "díky Svému milosrdenství, ó Nejmilostivější z milosrdných. Ámín, ó Pane všech světů."

### 2. SCRIPTURE & CITATIONS

*   **Quranic Citation Conventions:**
    *   Arabic texts quote the full vowelled verse followed by Surah name and verse number in brackets: e.g., `﴿يَعْلَمُ خَائِنَةَ الْأَعْيُنِ وَمَا تُخْفِي الصُّدُورُ﴾ [غافر: 19]` `[2026-11-06]`.
    *   Czech translations standardly adopt classical Czech Quranic phrasing (similar to I. Hrbek's translation register) and abbreviate citations to numerical chapter and verse format `(Surah:Verse)`, stripping the Arabic surah name: `„Alláh dobře zná proradnost očí i vše, co v hrudích je skryto.“ (40:19)` `[2026-11-06]`.
    *   *Inline Context Addition:* In `[2026-09-25]`, where Arabic cites `[عبس: 1-4]`, Czech explicitly adds the Surah number and Czech translated name inline: `ze súry č. 80 – „Zakabonil se“ (80:1-4)`.
*   **Hadith Attestation Formulae:**
    Hadith sources are consistently converted from Arabic verbal attribution to a Czech prepositional formula `(podle [Author])`:
    *   `رواه الترمذي` $\rightarrow$ `(podle at-Tirmídhího)` `[2026-11-06]`
    *   `رواه مسلم` $\rightarrow$ `(podle Muslima)` `[2026-10-23]`
    *   `رواه أحمد` $\rightarrow$ `(podle Ahmeda)` `[2026-10-30]` / `(podle imáma Ahmada)` `[2026-09-18]`
    *   `رواه البخاري ومسلم` $\rightarrow$ `(podle al-Buchárího a Muslima)` `[2026-10-30]`
    *   `متفق عليه` is consistently rendered as `(všichni se na něm shodují)` `[2026-10-30]`, `[2026-10-23]`.

### 3. AUDIENCE, TONE & RHETORICAL EDITING

*   **Gender Neutralization & Explicit Inclusivity:**
    The Arabic texts use standard masculine plural address forms (`أيها المؤمنون`, `عباد الله`, `أيها الإخوة`), whereas the Czech text systematically expands these to explicitly address female congregants:
    *   `[2026-11-06]`: AR `أيها الإخوة المؤمنون` ("O believing brothers") $\rightarrow$ CS `Bratři a sestry`.
    *   `[2026-11-06]`: AR `ستر أختك / أخيك` context $\rightarrow$ CS `odhalili tajemství svého bratra či sestry`.
    *   `[2026-10-30]`: AR `أيها الأحبة في الله` $\rightarrow$ CS `Milí bratři a sestry`.
    *   `[2026-10-23]`: AR `أيها الإخوة الكرام` $\rightarrow$ CS `Vážení bratři a sestry`.
*   **Omission of Arabic Poetic Stanzas:**
    In `[2026-09-04]`, the Arabic text contains four separate two-line poetic couplets used as rhetorical flourishes. Every single poetic stanza is completely omitted in the Czech translation:
    *   Omitted stanza 1: `يَا خَجَلَتِي مِنْ عِتَابِ اللهِ يَوْمَ غَدٍ / إِنْ قَالَ: خَالَفْتَ أَمْرِي أَيُّهَا الرَّجُلُ!...`
    *   Omitted stanza 2: `يا من يراني على ذنبي فيمهلني / جد لي بعفوك إن الذنب أشقاني...`
    *   Omitted stanza 3: `قم في الدجى واتلو الكتاب ولا تنم / إلا كنومة حائر ولهان...`
    *   Omitted stanza 4: `رفعت إلي رب الأنام مطالبي / ووجهت وجهي نحوه ورغائبي...`
*   **Textual Streamlining and Condensation:**
    *   `[2026-10-16]`: AR enumerates details ("تتسع دائرة الحروب والصراعات، وترتفع تكاليف المعيشة والضغوط الاقتصادية والاجتماعية، كما يزداد قلق كثير من المسلمين في أوروبا على دينهم...") while CS condenses this into a single clause (`Mezi charakteristické rysy naší doby patří četné události, války a ekonomické i sociální tlaky...`).
    *   `[2026-09-18]`: The dialogue of Walid bin 'Ubada visiting his father is streamlined in Czech, maintaining core theological meaning while shortening conversational fillers.

### 4. TERMINOLOGY & CULTURAL ADAPTATION

*   **Transliteration vs. Inline Glossing:**
    *   **Eulogies:** `صلى الله عليه وسلم` is transliterated as `(salla lláhu ʻalajhi wa sallam)`. Companions' honorific `رضي الله عنه` is transliterated phonetically.
    *   **Legal / Ethical Terms:**
        *   `[2026-10-09]`: AR `الحرام` / `الحلال` are translated with inline glosses: `„To je harám – zakázané“` and `to, co je halál – povolené`.
        *   `[2026-10-02]`: The core Quranic concept of *Taffif* (cheating measures) is translated functionally depending on context as `krácení míry` (shortening measures) or `podvádění` (cheating/fraud).
*   **Eschatological Terms:**
    *   `اليوم الآخر` / `الآخرة` are rendered consistently as `Onen svět`, `posmrtný život`, or `Soudný den`.
    *   `الجنة` is translated directly as `Ráj`.
