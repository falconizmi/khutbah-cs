# Batch 07 Discovery (2021-08-27 .. 2021-11-12) — Model: gemini-3.6-flash

An objective linguistic analysis of the 10 parallel Arabic–Czech Friday sermon texts delivered by Dr. Usama al-Diri at the Prague Mosque between August and November 2021.

---

### 1. STRUCTURE & FRAMING

*   **Standardized Czech Opening Prelude:** Across all 10 sermon pairs, the Arabic text starts directly with the topic or opening thematic paragraph, whereas the Czech text consistently inserts a standardized, formal liturgical Khutbah prelude containing the Hamdala, Shahada, honorifics, and Quran 33:70–71:
    > *Czech Prelude:* "Chvála Alláhu. Jeho uctíváme a Jeho o pomoc prosíme. Dosvědčuji, že není božstva kromě Alláha... „Vy, kteří jste uvěřili, chraňte se před trestem Alláha a říkejte slova správná!...“" `[All Sermons]`
*   **Hijri Date Insertion:** The Arabic header provides only the Gregorian date (or omits the Hijri conversion), while the Czech header systematically calculates and appends the exact Hijri calendar date in parentheses:
    *   `[2021-11-12]`: `(7. rab´II. 1443 H)`
    *   `[2021-11-05]`: `(30. rab´I. 1443 H)`
    *   `[2021-10-01]`: `(24. safar 1443 H)`
    *   `[2021-08-27]`: `(19. muharram 1443 H)`
*   **Closing Supplications (Du'a):** When the Arabic text includes a brief closing prayer, the Czech text translates it directly. Where absent in Arabic, Czech sometimes adds standard invocations:
    *   `[2021-10-29]`: AR omits closing du'a; CS adds: *"Ó Alláhu, ochraňuj Své služebníky a všechny země a dopřej nám dobrého konce."*
    *   `[2021-09-24]`: AR `اللهم احفظنا واحفظ اولادنا...` -> CS *"Ó Alláhu, ochraňuj nás, ochraňuj naše děti..."*
*   **Section Markers:** Explicit structural list markers in Arabic (e.g., "الإشارة الأولى", "الإشارة الثانية") are rendered systematically into Czech ordinal references ("První odkaz", "Druhý odkaz") `[2021-11-12]`.

### 2. SCRIPTURE & CITATIONS

*   **Quran Citation References:**
    *   **In-text verse numbering:** The Arabic text embeds Quranic verses directly without inline chapter/verse numbers. The Czech translator systematically inserts precise parenthetical surah and verse numbers following standard Czech conventions:
        *   `[2021-11-12]`: AR `{يأيها الناس اتقوا ربكم...}` -> CS `„Lidé, bojte se Pána svého...“ (4:1)`
        *   `[2021-10-29]`: CS adds `(49:1)`, `(49:2)`, `(49:6)`, `(49:7)`, `(49:11)`, `(49:12)`, `(49:13)`.
*   **Translation Source for Quran:** Czech Quranic passages strictly adhere to **Ivan Hrbek’s standard Czech translation** (1972), retaining archaic phrasing, inverted syntax, and specific terminology (e.g., "bytosti jediné", "v množství velké mužů i žen").
*   **Hadith Source Attribution & Grading Notes:**
    *   `متفق عليه` (Agreed upon by Bukhari & Muslim) is translated verbatim as *"všichni se na něm shodují"* `[2021-10-15]`, `[2021-10-08]`, `[2021-10-01]`.
    *   `صحيح البخاري` -> *"al-Buchárého sbírka správných hadísů"* `[2021-10-01]`.
    *   `رواه الطبراني` -> *"podle at-Tabarího"* `[2021-09-17]`.
    *   `مسند البزار` -> *"ve sbírce al-Bazár"* `[2021-10-22]`.
    *   `مسند الإمام أحمد` -> *"v tradici imáma Ahmeda"* `[2021-10-22]`.

### 3. AUDIENCE, TONE & RHETORICAL EDITING

*   **Audience Address Forms:**
    *   `عباد الله` ("O servants of Allah") -> translated literally into Czech as *"Služebníci Alláhovi"* `[2021-10-22]`, `[2021-10-08]`, `[2021-09-24]`.
    *   `أيها الإخوة المؤمنون` ("O believing brothers") -> adapted to *"Bratři muslimové"* `[2021-10-29]`.
    *   `أخي الكريم` ("My noble brother") -> translated as *"vážení bratři"* (pluralized in Czech) `[2021-08-27]`.
*   **Paraphrasing and Semantic Shifts:**
    *   `[2021-10-22]`: AR `نصراً حسيّا ... ومعنوياً` ("a physical/sensory victory ... and a moral/spiritual victory") is translated into Czech as *"emocionální vítězství ... morální vítězství"* ("an emotional victory...").
    *   `[2021-11-12]`: Hadith phrase `وفي بضع أحدكم صدقة` (marital intimacy is charity) is rendered directly and explicitly as *"Vzájemné uspokojování je dobročinným darem"* ("Mutual gratification is a charitable gift").
    *   `[2021-10-01]`: `إماطة الأذى عن الطريق صدقة` -> *"Odstranění nebezpečí z cesty je dobročinností"* ("Removing danger from the path is charity").

### 5. SURPRISES, EDGE CASES & HUMAN QUIRKS

#### A. Truncated Text Error (Human Publishing Mistake)
*   `[2021-10-01]`: The Czech sermon text ends abruptly mid-word at the very last sentence:
    *   **Arabic Original:** `وإذا علّمه لغيره فإنه من العلم النافع الذي يستمر بعد موته.`
    *   **Czech Text:** `...které bude trvat i po jeho sm` *(truncates mid-word before finishing `smrti`!)*

#### B. Header Date Discrepancies & Typographical Errors in Arabic
*   `[2021-10-29]`: The Arabic header contains a typographical error in the date (`2021/10/19`), whereas the Czech header correctly gives the true Friday date (`29. 10. 2021`).
*   `[2021-10-22]`: The Arabic header contains a typo omitting the slash: `(خطبة الجمعة 2021/1022}` instead of `2021/10/22`.

#### C. Arabic Spelling Error Corrected in Czech Translation
*   `[2021-11-12]`: The Arabic text misspells Hadith compiler Al-Tirmidhi as `الترمدي` (with `د` instead of `ذ`). The Czech translator quietly corrects this mistake in transliteration to `(At-Tirmídhí)`.

#### D. Literal Translation of a Harsh Military Metaphor
*   `[2021-11-05]`: The Arabic text describes a righteous woman as an active spiritual asset: `هي ذخيرة حية ينتصر بها القيم والأخلاق` (*"live reserve/ammunition through which values win"*). The Czech text translates `ذخيرة حية` literally as *"ostrým nábojem"* ("a live round / sharp cartridge"):
    > *Czech:* "každá žena, která se snaží o zlepšení ve společnosti, **je ostrým nábojem**, díky kterému vítězí hodnoty a správná morálka..."

#### E. Traditional Biblical Formula for "Kun Fayakun"
*   `[2021-11-12]`: The Quranic formula `{كن فيكون}` (Be, and it is) is rendered in Czech using the archaic/biblical phrase: `„Staniž se. A stalo se.“`
