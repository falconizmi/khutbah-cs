### 1. STRUCTURE & FRAMING
*   **Titles and Headers:** Arabic titles are phrased as descriptive topics (e.g., `فضائل شهر محرم – خطبة الجمعة (2022/8/5م)` [2022-08-05]), while Czech headers standardize this into a clean format with Czech translations, dates, and sometimes Hijri equivalents: `Přednosti měsíce muharramu: 5. 8. 2022 (7. muharram 1444 H)` [2022-08-05].
*   **Liturgical Openings:** The Czech texts consistently insert a standardized introductory Khutbat al-Hajah block (*Chvála Alláhu. Jeho uctíváme... Dosvědčuji, že není božstva...*), even when the Arabic source text begins directly with the sermon’s specific thematic introduction without the full traditional invocation (e.g., `جعل الله -سبحانه وتعالى- لنا مواسم...` [2022-08-05]).
*   **Location Metadata:** The Czech framing explicitly tracks the physical location of delivery, alternating between `Mešita v Praze` [2022-08-05] and `Mešita v Teplicích` [2022-07-15], which is absent from the Arabic source headers.

### 2. SCRIPTURE & CITATIONS
*   **Quranic Formatting:** Arabic texts use traditional Quranic brackets and script (e.g., `(إِنَّ عِدَّةَ الشُّهُورِ...)` [2022-08-05] or `﴿وَسِعَ كُرْسِيُّهُ...﴾` [2022-05-13]). The Czech translations integrate these as direct quotes enclosed in quotation marks, frequently appending standard surah:ayah citations in parentheses, such as `(9:36)` [2022-08-05] and `(2:255)` [2022-05-13].
*   **Hadith Attribution:** Arabic introductions like `فعن أبي بكرة -رضي الله عنه- عن النبي -صلى الله عليه وسلم- قال:` [2022-08-05] are adapted smoothly into fluent Czech narrative phrasing: `jak tradoval Abú Bakr (rAa) slova Proroka (salla lláhu ʻalajhi wa sallam):` [2022-08-05].
*   **Omission of Hadith Grading:** Technical scholarly gradings present in the Arabic source are occasionally dropped entirely in the Czech version. For instance, in `[2022-05-20]`, the Arabic text includes explicit chain/grading notes: `صحيح – رواه أحمد في “المسند”`, which the Czech text completely omits, retaining only the prophetic text.

### 3. AUDIENCE, TONE & RHETORICAL EDITING
*   **Direct Address and Paragraphing:** The Czech translator frequently breaks long, dense Arabic paragraphs into shorter, digestible blocks for the Czech congregation. Direct addresses are adapted to fit Czech rhetorical style, such as translating `احبتي في الله` into `Milí bratři a sestry` [2022-05-02] or opening personal segments with `Moje dnešní páteční promluva k vám...` [2022-05-20].

*   **Transliteration vs. Translation:** Islamic technical terms are handled contextually.
