## 1. Executive Summary & Universal Patterns (2021–2026)

Across all 7 empirical batch investigations covering 70 parallel sermon pairs spanning 2021 to 2026, the translation methodology of the Prague Mosque exhibits a highly standardized, institutionalized workflow. The Arabic source texts serve as live spoken transcripts or thematic drafts, whereas the Czech translations are systematically edited, structured, and contextualized for spoken delivery to a linguistically diverse European Muslim congregation.

### Universal Invariants (100% Consistency Across Corpus)
1. **Mandatory Standardized Liturgical Opening Prelude (*Khutbat al-Hājah*):**
   * *Arabic Source:* Begins immediately with thematic introductions, historical narratives, or direct quotes, omitting top formal doxologies in the written text.
   * *Czech Translation:* **100% of Czech sermons** insert a fixed opening header block containing a standardized Czech rendering of *Khutbat al-Hājah* combined with Qur'an 33:70–71 (and occasionally 3:102).
2. **Calendar Dual-Dating:**
   * *Arabic Source:* Provides Gregorian dates only, occasionally with typos or missing markers.
   * *Czech Translation:* Systematically standardizes the Gregorian date format (`DD. MM. YYYY`) and calculates/appends the precise Hijri calendar date in parentheses (e.g., `6. 11. 2026 (26. džumáda al-awwal 1448 h)`).
3. **Qur'anic Citation Standard:**
   * *Arabic Source:* Uses traditional vowelled script in decorative brackets `﴿...﴾` followed by textual surah names in brackets `[غافر: 19]`.
   * *Czech Translation:* Replaces text with elevated Czech Qur'anic register (derived strictly from Ivan Hrbek’s 1972 translation), enclosed in Czech quotation marks `„...“` and ending with parenthetical numerical chapter-and-verse notation `(40:19)`. Surah names are stripped out unless explicitly required by narrative context.
4. **Omission of Poetic Stanzas:**
   * *Arabic Source:* Frequently incorporates classical two-line poetic couplets (`شعر`) as rhetorical flourishes.
   * *Czech Translation:* **100% of poetic stanzas are omitted** in the Czech versions to preserve clarity and avoid stylistic dissonance in spoken Czech.
5. **Gender-Inclusive Direct Address:**
   * *Arabic Source:* Standard masculine plural vocatives (`أيها الإخوة`, `عباد الله`, `أيها المسلمون`).
   * *Czech Translation:* Consistently expanded to gender-inclusive doublets (`Bratři a sestry`, `Muslimové a muslimky`, `Vážení bratři a sestry`).
6. **Transliterated Honorific Eulogies:**
   * Prophetic and Companion honorifics are never translated into native Czech prose; they are strictly transliterated in Latin script within hyphens or parentheses: `(salla lláhu ʻalajhi wa sallam)`, `– radija lláhu anhu –`.

## 2. Variabilities & Editorial Adaptation Spectrum

While core liturgical elements are invariant, editorial freedom varies based on subject matter, sermon length, and complexity:

| Dimension | Standard Faithful Register | Condensed / Editorial Register |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Structural Formatting** | Direct paragraph-for-paragraph translation maintaining narrative flow. | Reformatting continuous Arabic prose into explicit numbered lists (`1) ... 2) ...`), or conversely, flattening complex 3-tiered lists into running paragraphs. |
| **Hadith Isnād & Authentication** | Converts attestation to prepositional phrases: `رواه مسلم` $\rightarrow$ `(podle Muslima)`; `متفق عليه` $\rightarrow$ `(všichni se na něm shodují)`. | Complete stripping of Hadith chains and technical grading notes (e.g., dropping *bisandin ṣaḥīḥ* or *bisandin ḥasan*). |
| **Geopolitical & Local References** | Retains European/Czech contextual remarks (`v našem evropském kontextu`). | Suppresses specific geopolitical supplications (e.g., omitting Gaza/Palestine closing prayers in `[2026-01-02]`) or localized anecdotes. |
| **Proper Naming Conventions** | Uses traditional Czech biblical equivalents for shared prophets (Mojžíš, Marie, Josef, Adam, Eva). | Retains direct Arabic transliterations for Islamic historical figures (Ibráhím, Abú Bakr, Umar bin al-Chattáb, Chadídža). |

## 3. Verified Liturgical Formulas & Terminology Index

### A. Opening Liturgical Header (Standard Czech *Khutbat al-Hājah*)
Used as the mandatory header across all 70 sermons:

> **Páteční kázání [DD. MM. YYYY] ([Hijri Day. Hijri Month YYYY h])**  
> **[Czech Title]**  
> 
> Chvála Alláhu. Jeho uctíváme a Jeho o pomoc prosíme. Dosvědčuji, že není božstva kromě Alláha a že Muhammad (salla lláhu ʻalajhi wa sallam) je Jeho služebník a posel. „Vy, kteří jste uvěřili, chraňte se před trestem Alláha a říkejte slova správná! On napraví vám vaše skutky a odpustí vám vaše hříchy; a ten, kdo poslechne Alláha a posla Jeho, ten dosáhne nesmírného vítězství.“

* `أيها الإخوة المؤمنون` / `أيها الإخوة الكرام` $\rightarrow$ **Bratři a sestry,** / **Milí bratři a sestry,**
* `عباد الله` $\rightarrow$ **Služebníci Alláhovi,**
* `أيها المسلمون` $\rightarrow$ **Muslimové a muslimky,** / **Vážení muslimové,**
* `أيها الآباء والأمهات` $\rightarrow$ **Vážení rodiče,** / **Otcové a matky,**

### C. Honorific Formulae
* **Prophet Muhammad:** `(salla lláhu ʻalajhi wa sallam)` or `– salla lláhu alajhi wa sallam –`
* **Male Companion:** `– radija lláhu anhu –` or `(radija lláhu anhu)`
* **Female Companion:** `– radija lláhu anhá –`
* **Plural Companions:** `– radija lláhu anhum –`
* **Other Prophets:** `– alajhi as-salám –`
* **Deceased Scholars:** `– nechť je k němu Alláh milosrdný –` *(for رحمه الله)*

### D. Hadith Attribution Formulas
* `رواه البخاري` $\rightarrow$ `(podle al-Buchárího)`
* `رواه مسلم` $\rightarrow$ `(podle Muslima)`

* `آمين يا رب العالمين` $\rightarrow$ **„Ámín, ó Pane všech světů.“** / **„Ámín.“**

## 4. Human Quirks & Errors Identified in Corpus (DO NOT COPY)

An automated AI translation system **must not replicate** the following human editorial errors spotted in the 2021–2026 archive:

1. **Hadith Grading Terminology Error (`[2026-11-06]`):**
   * *Source Arabic:* `وحسنه بعض أهل العلم` (*"and some scholars graded it good/ḥasan"*).
   * *Human Error:* `někteří učenci tento hadís upravili` (*"some scholars edited/adjusted this hadith"*).
   * *Rule:* Never translate *ḥassana* (authenticated as good) as "edited" or "modified".
2. **Text Truncation Glitch (`[2021-10-01]`):**
   * *Human Error:* Czech text truncates mid-word at sermon end: `...které bude trvat i po jeho sm` (incomplete for *smrti*).
   * *Rule:* Ensure all output sentences are grammatically complete.
3. **Unintended Paragraph Duplication (`[2026-08-28]`):**
   * *Human Error:* A draft revision resulted in a duplicated 50-word sentence in the published Czech text.
   * *Rule:* Enforce strict output deduplication.
4. **Numerical Citation Transposition (`[2026-07-24]`):**
   * *Source Arabic:* Surah Al-An'am verse 148 `[الأنعام: 148]`.
   * *Human Error:* Czech citation mislabeled as `(6:143)`.
   * *Rule:* Verify numerical verse alignment against the Arabic source text.
5. **Typographical Errors in Dates & Months:**
   * *Human Error:* Headers containing standalone acute accents before letters (e.g., `š´abán` in Batch 04) or missing slashes in Arabic dates (`2021/10122`).
   * *Rule:* Use correct Czech diacritics and standard apostrophes (`ša'bán`).
6. **Czech Grammatical Agreement Oversights:**
   * *Human Errors:* `země... nedokázali` (feminine plural noun matched with masculine animate verb) and `spousta... přišlo`.
   * *Rule:* Standardize high-register Czech grammar and gender agreement.
